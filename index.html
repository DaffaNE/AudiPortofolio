01 — The through-line

Good operations are invisible. Until they are not.

A management graduate who found her footing in the details other people skip.

Monika Claudia Sarita · Tangerang Selatan

I started in social media — photographing products, writing captions, and watching which posts actually moved people. That curiosity about why numbers change led me into operations, where the answers are far less glamorous and much more useful.

Since then I have reconciled petty cash in a pharmaceutical company, kept TikTok, Shopee, and Tokopedia storefronts consistent, cleaned up product data for AEON, and built the daily reporting rhythm a small team depends on. The pattern is the same each time: find what is messy, give it a structure, document it, and make it survive without me.

I am a Cum Laude management graduate from Universitas Pembangunan Jaya, and a published researcher on brand loyalty and consumer behaviour. I like work where precision compounds.

“I do not just finish the task. I leave behind a process the next person can follow.”
